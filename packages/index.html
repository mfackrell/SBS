How pricing works

What does "starting at" mean?
Your fee depends on transaction volume, number of accounts, sales channels, entities, payroll and AP activity, and the condition of your books. We confirm your price in writing after your review.
How do you count transactions?
For ecommerce, we count payout-level summaries from each platform, not individual orders, so a busy store isn't penalized for order count. Other businesses are counted by bank and card transactions.
What if I go over my plan's volume?
Overages are quoted in 50-transaction blocks, agreed in your engagement letter. We'll also tell you if it makes sense to move up a tier.
When will my books be closed each month?
We document your close timeline at onboarding, based on your business and your records. Timing depends on receiving complete records from you.
Are software subscriptions included?
No. QuickBooks, Xero, and related tools are billed separately and stay in your name.
Is cleanup included?
Catch-up and cleanup work is scoped and quoted separately.
What's the term?
Plans begin with a 90-day initial term, then continue month to month with 30 days' notice.
Do you offer other services?
Yes: payroll processing support, AP bill management, extra channel or entity reconciliation, budgeting and forecasting, onboarding, and fractional CFO consulting. All are scoped and quoted.
Do you handle sales tax filings?
We track sales-tax liability and coordinate with your CPA or filing service.
