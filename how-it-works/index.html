How SBS works

A monthly accounting process you can actually see.

We set up the close once, run it every month, and give you a secure workspace so you always know what SBS needs, what stage the close is in, and what has been delivered.

1

We understand your books.

We assess the current state of your accounting, systems, transaction flow, reporting gaps and monthly-close process.

2

We build the monthly process.

We confirm responsibilities, access, chart of accounts, recurring records, close procedures, review steps and reporting expectations.

3

You get your SBS workspace.

Your secure client portal becomes the central place for document requests, file exchange, communication, proposals, billing access and monthly close status.

Explore the SBS Client Portal

4

We close and review every month.

Records come in, accounts are reconciled, the close checklist is completed, the work is reviewed, and your financial statements are delivered.

5

You always know what is happening.

See outstanding requests and close status without chasing your accountant, then use the reporting and review conversation to understand what the numbers mean.

What we need from you

Read-only access to your bank and card feeds and accounting software, plus timely delivery of the records requested through your SBS workspace.
